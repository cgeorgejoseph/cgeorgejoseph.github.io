Philosophy
Resilience and antifragility aren't just research interests — they're the lens through which I understand organizations, markets, and people. — George Joseph, Ph.D.

Dr. George Joseph is an Assistant Professor at Rajagiri Business School (AACSB Accredited), Kochi, India, where he leads the Incubation Centre and the Institutional Innovation Council. He holds a Ph.D. in Operational Flexibility from CUSAT, an MBA, and a B.Tech in Engineering.

His work spans academic publishing, industry consulting, curriculum design, and international research collaboration — including a Visiting Research Scholar appointment at WFI Ingolstadt School of Management, Katholische Universität Eichstätt-Ingolstadt, Germany supported by a Bayerische Forschungsallianz grant.

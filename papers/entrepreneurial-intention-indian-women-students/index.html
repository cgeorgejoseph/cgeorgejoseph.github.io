What shapes the entrepreneurial intention of women university students in India?

Entrepreneurial intention of Indian women university students: the role of entrepreneurial self-assessment and entrepreneurial passion

Neenet Baby Manjaly, George Joseph, Kailash Nadh K.S. · Journal of Women's Entrepreneurship and Education, No. 3-4, 273-290, 2022

What the paper found

Women students who assess their own entrepreneurial capability more favourably report stronger intention to start a venture. The paper proposes that entrepreneurial passion mediates this link between self-assessment and intention.

How the study was done

An empirical study of women university students in India, testing a model that connects entrepreneurial self-assessment, passion and intention.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on women's entrepreneurship, entrepreneurial intention, entrepreneurial passion, self-assessment and entrepreneurship education in emerging markets.

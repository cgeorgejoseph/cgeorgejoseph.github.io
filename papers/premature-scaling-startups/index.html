Why do startup founders scale too early?

Entrepreneurial cognition and premature scaling of startups: a qualitative analysis of determinants of start-up failures

George Joseph, Nimitha Aboobaker, Zakkariya K.A. · Journal of Entrepreneurship in Emerging Economies, 15(1), 96-112, 2023

Emerald Literati Outstanding Paper Award, 2024

What the paper found

Premature scaling is traced here to how entrepreneurs think and feel when they decide, not only to the business idea. Six patterns came out of the interviews: comparison with others, emotional over-reaction, impatience, mistaken customer priorities, overestimation and overconfidence.

How the study was done

In-depth semi-structured interviews with eight participants reached through snowball sampling, analysed thematically. The paper reads the results through self-regulation attitude theory.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on startup failure, entrepreneurial cognition, decision-making heuristics, cognitive bias in founders and the sustainable growth of technology startups.

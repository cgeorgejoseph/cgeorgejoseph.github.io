How are mobile phones changing agricultural supply chains in India?

Transformative power of mobile phones in the Indian agro-supply chain

George Joseph, K.A. Zakkariya · Digital Media and Wireless Communications in Developing Nations, CRC Press, 2019

What the paper found

Mobile services deliver four kinds of information that small farmers and agri-businesses need: know-how, contextual information such as weather, market prices and demand, and logistics. The chapter shows how this improves traceability, quality control, payments and supply chain efficiency, and it sets out the constraints, including local-language content, handset compatibility and illiteracy.

How the study was done

A review of mobile products and services across the stages of the agricultural value chain in India.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on agricultural supply chains, mobile technology for development, rural information services, mobile payments and smallholder farming.

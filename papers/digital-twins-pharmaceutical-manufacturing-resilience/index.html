How can digital twins make pharmaceutical manufacturing more resilient?

Embracing resilience in pharmaceutical manufacturing: "digital twins" - forging a resilient path in the VUCA maze

B. Avinash, George Joseph · International Journal of Pharmaceutical and Healthcare Marketing, 19(3), 750-772, 2025

What the paper found

The paper sets out strategies for strengthening resilience in pharmaceutical manufacturing under volatile, uncertain, complex and ambiguous (VUCA) conditions, with digital twins as the central technology. It treats resilience as the capability that lets manufacturers absorb disruption, and it proposes directions for future research.

How the study was done

A qualitative study built on an extensive review of scholarly articles, empirical studies and industry reports on manufacturing resilience and digital technology.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on digital twins, pharmaceutical supply chains, manufacturing resilience, Industry 4.0 in life sciences and operations under uncertainty.

Is the Kochi Water Metro a workable model for sustainable urban transport?

Assessing the sustainability and resilience of urban transit: the case of Kochi Water Metro

George Joseph, Arun A. Elias · Advances in Environmental Accounting & Management, Vol. 12, 141-157, 2024

What the paper found

The Kochi Water Metro improved connectivity and eased road congestion while keeping its carbon footprint low. The chapter also records economic gains through tourism, jobs and local livelihoods, and points to the project's adaptability to local conditions as part of what makes it sustainable.

How the study was done

A qualitative longitudinal case study using open-ended interviews, direct observation and qualitative content analysis. Data from the project's early years were limited.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on water transport, sustainable urban mobility, transit resilience, public transport policy in India and lessons for other cities.

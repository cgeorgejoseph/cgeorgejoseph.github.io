Does developing spirituality improve the well-being of management students?

Significance of developing spirituality among management students: discerning the impact on psychological resources and wellbeing

Nimmi P.M., Alka K. Binoy, George Joseph, R. Suma · Journal of Applied Research in Higher Education, 14(1), 317-331, 2022

What the paper found

Spirituality had a significant positive effect on the life well-being of management students. Two psychological resources, perceived employability and psychological capital, each carried part of that effect as mediators.

How the study was done

A cross-sectional survey of 212 management students in Kerala, India, selected by multi-stage random sampling and analysed with structural equation modelling. The results are discussed through broaden-and-build theory.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on student well-being, spirituality in higher education, psychological capital, perceived employability and management education in India.

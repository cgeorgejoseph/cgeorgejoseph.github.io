Why do Chinese contractors dominate construction markets in Southern Africa?

Organisational strategies for competitive advantage in the construction industry: Chinese dominance in Southern Africa

Daniel Soares Fernandes, George Joseph · Journal of Construction in Developing Countries, 25(2), 1-38, 2020

What the paper found

Three strategies stood out as contributing most to the success of Chinese multinational contractors: the ability to offer lower prices, easy access to loans and funds from their home government, and the ability to trade debt for local resources such as wood, land and minerals.

How the study was done

A mixed-methods study: an environmental analysis of Southern African construction markets, an organisational analysis of the main Chinese contractors, and an online questionnaire in which participants ranked the shortlisted strategies.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on international construction, emerging-market multinationals, competitive strategy, China-Africa business relations and state-backed finance.

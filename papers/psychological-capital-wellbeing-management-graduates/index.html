Management education and careers

Does psychological capital improve the life well-being of management graduates, and what role does perceived employability play?

Is it all about perception? A sustainability viewpoint on psychological capital and life well-being of management graduates

Nimmi P.M., George Joseph, William E. Donald · Higher Education, Skills and Work-Based Learning, 12(2), 384-398, 2022

What the paper found

Psychological capital and perceived employability are each positively related to life well-being among management students. Perceived employability also carries part of the effect: it mediates the link between psychological capital and well-being.

How the study was done

An empirical study of management students, framed around career sustainability during an uncertain academic and job-market period.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on graduate employability, sustainable careers, student well-being, positive psychological resources and the role of higher education institutions.

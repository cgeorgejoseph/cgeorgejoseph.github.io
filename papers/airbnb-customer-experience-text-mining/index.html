Services and customer experience

What do Airbnb guest reviews reveal about what drives customer satisfaction?

Analyzing Airbnb customer experience feedback using text mining

George Joseph, Vinu Varghese · Big Data and Innovation in Tourism, Travel, and Hospitality, 147-162, Springer, 2019

What the paper found

The chapter presents a text-mining case on Airbnb user reviews to identify the aspects of a stay that drive satisfaction. It discusses the difficulties customers face in the sharing economy and what it takes to develop highly rated hosts.

How the study was done

Text mining of Airbnb user reviews, presented as a worked case of big data analysis in tourism and hospitality.

Where it is relevant

Useful for work on the sharing economy, online reviews, customer experience, text analytics and hospitality management.
